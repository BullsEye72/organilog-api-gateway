# Code of conduct

## Expectations

Everyone participating in this project should be treated with respect, regardless
of experience, background, identity, or personal characteristics.

- Be considerate, patient, and welcoming to people learning the project.
- Give constructive feedback about the work and respect differing viewpoints.
- Assume good intent, accept corrections, and take responsibility for your impact.
- Respect people's privacy and keep discussion relevant to the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention or content, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption, and publishing someone's private information without
permission are unacceptable.

## Scope

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project spaces, as well as when someone publicly represents the project.

## Reporting

The repository maintainer, [@BullsEye72](https://github.com/BullsEye72), handles
conduct reports. Use a private contact method listed on that profile. If none is
available, open an issue asking only for a private contact method, without naming
people or describing the incident publicly. Do not use security advisories for
conduct reports; suspected software vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Include links, relevant context, and the outcome you are seeking in your private
report. Share only information needed to assess it. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as possible, with information shared only as needed to address the
incident. Response times depend on maintainer availability.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content and may warn participants,
restrict participation temporarily, or exclude participants from project spaces.
The response will take the severity, context, and any repeated behavior into
account. Anyone affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration
through the same private contact method.
